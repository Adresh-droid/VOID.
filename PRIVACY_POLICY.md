# Privacy Policy for VOID Music Player

**Last Updated:** September 29, 2026

## 1. Introduction
**VOID Music Player** ("VOID", "we", "our", or "us") is an open-source, non-profit Android music player designed with user privacy as a core principle. This Privacy Policy explains how VOID handles user data.

## 2. No Data Collection or Tracking
- **Zero Telemetry:** VOID does not collect, record, track, or sell any personal information, location data, device identifiers, or usage metrics.
- **No Third-Party Analytics:** We do not include third-party analytics SDKs, tracking pixels, or advertising networks.

## 3. Network Requests & Music Streaming
- **YouTube / InnerTube API:** VOID streams audio and video content directly from YouTube Music / InnerTube servers over encrypted HTTPS endpoints.
- **Direct Communication:** All network requests travel directly from your device to Google/YouTube servers without passing through any intermediate proxy or tracking servers owned by VOID.

## 4. Local Data Storage & Caching
- **App-Private Storage:** Downloaded audio tracks, video stream caches, and lyrics are saved exclusively in your device's app-private storage (`playerCache`).
- **Database Security:** Playlists, song history, and settings are saved locally in a Room SQLite database on your phone.

## 5. Third-Party Links & Services
- Optional integrations (such as Spotify playlist import or Last.fm scrobbling) interact directly with their respective official API endpoints using user-authenticated sessions.
