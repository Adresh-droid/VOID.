## ✨ Features

- 🎥 **Full 9:16 Video Canvas**: Stream YouTube official music videos natively rendered as edge-to-edge background canvases without letterboxing or horizontal cramping.
- 🧊 **60 FPS 3D Album Motion**: Interactive gyroscopic 3D tilt artwork that responds to your device motion in real-time.
- 🎛️ **Audiophile DSP Engine**: Built-in 10-band Parametric Equalizer, Schroeder algorithmic Reverb, and soft-knee Compression processor.
- 🚀 **Next-Song Pre-Fetching**: Secretly pre-resolves upcoming track video streams for 0-latency gapless track switching.
- 💾 **Disk Video Caching**: Stores background video chunks locally in `playerCache` so replaying songs consumes zero network data.
- 🎨 **Appearance Customization**: Light Mode, Dark Mode, AMOLED Pure Black, and 20+ vibrant accent color palettes (`#C528D5` Cyber Purple).
- 🎵 **Spotify Import & Local Sync**: Mirror playlists from Spotify or YouTube Music directly into Room SQLite storage.
