📋 Version Changelog

Version 0.1.9

September 28, 2026
  • Fixed Online & Recent Playlists: Resolved 404/VL prefix handling so online playlists load live tracks and song counts instantly.
  • Fixed Audio Chopping & Stutter: Re-architected EQ, Reverb, and Compressor audio processors to eliminate on-thread allocations.
  • No-Fail Video Quality Selector: Added 1080p, 720p, 480p, and 360p video quality options under Settings.
  • Android 10/11 GPU Blur Protection: Safe fallback for pre-Android 12 devices to prevent GPU rendering lag.
  • Queue Deduplication: Fixed issue where starting a track duplicated the song in the queue.
