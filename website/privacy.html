Privacy Policy for VOID Music Player

Last Updated: September 29, 2026

1. Introduction

VOID Music Player is an open-source, non-profit Android music player designed with user privacy as a core principle.

2. No Data Collection or Tracking

VOID does not collect, record, track, or sell any personal information, location data, or device identifiers. There are no third-party analytics SDKs or advertising networks included.

3. Encrypted Music Streaming

All network requests travel directly from your Android device to YouTube Music / InnerTube servers over encrypted HTTPS without passing through any intermediate proxy servers owned by VOID.

4. Local Device Storage

Downloaded audio, video caches, and lyrics are saved exclusively in your phone's app-private storage. Playlists and settings remain local on your device.
